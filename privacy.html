Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026
Effective for traders in East Africa (Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, Burundi, South Sudan).
NO FINANCIAL ADVICE

Tidetic is a performance tracking and analytics tool. We do not provide financial, investment, or trading advice. All insights, scores, and observations generated by the app are purely statistical analyses based solely on your own historical trading data. You are solely responsible for your trading decisions and risk management.

1. INTRODUCTION

Tidetic is committed to protecting the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, use, and protect your information in compliance with East African data protection regulations, including the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019, the Uganda Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019, and the Tanzania Personal Data Protection Act 2022.

2. DATA WE COLLECT

To provide you with high-quality analytics, streaks, and journaling services, we collect:

  • Account Profile: Email, display name, and optional profile image.
  • Trading Records: Account balances, currency pairs, entry/exit prices, timestamps, performance metrics, and logs.
  • App Settings: Notification preferences, local cache, and biometric authentication status.

3. BILLING & M-PESA PAYMENTS

All premium subscriptions are processed securely using Safaricom Daraja (M-Pesa). We facilitate payments via Mobile Money.

Important Safety Notice:
• Tidetic does NOT access, process, or store your Mobile Money PINs, credit card numbers, CVVs, or payment passwords.
• All transaction credentials are sent directly and securely to Safaricom Daraja’s encrypted tokenization server.
• We only receive confirmation of payment status (e.g., success, failure) and subscription references to unlock your features.

4. DATA STORAGE & SYNC

Your trading journal and records may be cached locally on your device for offline productivity. When online, your records are synchronized securely to Supabase servers. We use industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS) for data in transit and encryption at rest where available to reduce the risk of unauthorized access.

5. YOUR REGIONAL RIGHTS

Under regional data privacy laws, you possess the right to:

  • Access and request a copy of all your logged trading data.
  • Correct or update inaccuracies in your profile and trading accounts.
  • Delete your account and purge all trade history permanently from our databases.

You can execute these actions directly within the settings screen or by contacting support.

6. DATA RETENTION

We retain your data for as long as your account remains active so the journal and analytics can work. You may request deletion at any time; when you delete your account, we permanently remove your profile and associated journal data from our systems within a reasonable period, subject to legal requirements.

7. COOKIES & TRACKING

Tidetic does not use third‑party advertising SDKs. We may use essential analytics to understand app stability and performance. We do not sell your personal data.

8. CONTACT

If you have questions, requests, or complaints regarding your privacy rights under the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019, Uganda Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019, or Tanzania Personal Data Protection Act 2022, contact us at tideticjournal@gmail.com.
